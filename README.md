# Talentwarden

<img src="docs/logo.png" alt="Talentwarden icon" width="96" align="right">

Tells you where to spend your next talent point in WoW Forever.

- Level-by-level leveling guides (levels 10-60) and endgame builds for all 27 specs.
- A window with your spec, the build you follow, the next talent point and the steps coming up.
- Glows the next talent in the talent window.
- Chat reminder when you have points to spend, and a warning when you go off the build.
- Make builds of your own, and change everything in the options.
- Right-click the minimap button to switch builds.
- Look system: fonts, colors and textures to restyle the window.

Build data is based on the Icy Veins WoW Forever talent guides.
