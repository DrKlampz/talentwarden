# Changelog

## v0.2.2
- Look sync: the first time your add-ons meet, they now all take on the same look right away (Baggie's, if it is installed) instead of waiting for you to change something. Baggie no longer shares its title bar color, which means something different there.

## v0.2.1
- After you reset all your talents, every talent in your plan now glows with a number on it: the points that talent should get (as many points as you can spend). Switch it off in the options with "After a reset, glow every planned talent with its point count".
- Your custom look is now shared with your other add-ons: change colors, background, border or opacity in one and the rest follow (the newest change wins). It is on by default; untick "Sync my look with my other add-ons" in the Look window to turn it off.
- The window now raises when clicked and sits above HUD elements instead of under them.

## v0.2.0
- First public release: next-talent guidance for every class and spec, talent window glow, off-build warning, custom builds and the Look system.
